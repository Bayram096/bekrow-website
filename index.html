FAQ

Common questions

What does Bekrow Development do?

Bekrow Development Ltd. is a commercial general contractor in Metro Vancouver, BC. We build tenant improvements for offices, bank branches, healthcare clinics and retail, and also offer general contracting and project management.

Which areas do you serve?

Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley, including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, Coquitlam, North Vancouver, New Westminster, Delta, Langley, White Rock, Port Coquitlam and Abbotsford. Larger commercial projects are considered across BC.

Can you build while our business stays open?

Yes. We specialize in occupied spaces, with after-hours and phased work, dust and noise control, so your doors can stay open.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We manage permits, subtrades, inspections, deficiencies and closeout, with one point of contact from start to finish.

Are you licensed and insured?

Bekrow Development is WorkSafeBC registered and fully insured with commercial general liability (CGL) coverage.
